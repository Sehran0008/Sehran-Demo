Iron Man

Tony Stark is a brilliant inventor, engineer, and wealthy industrialist who runs Stark Industries. While demonstrating weapons in a war zone, he is seriously injured and captured. Shrapnel lodged near his heart threatens his life. To survive, Stark and fellow captive Ho Yinsen build a device to keep his heart functioning. Secretly, they also create a powered suit of armor. Using the suit, Stark escapes captivity. After returning home, he improves the armor and decides to use his technology to protect people rather than create more destructive weapons. He becomes Iron Man.

Fictional character biography

Iron Man, whose real name is Tony Stark, is a fictional superhero appearing in comics published by Marvel Comics. A brilliant inventor, engineer, and businessman, Stark uses advanced technology and powered armor to protect the world as Iron Man. Tony Stark was born to Howard Stark and Maria Stark, the wealthy owners of Stark Industries. A child prodigy, he showed exceptional talent in science and engineering from a young age. After his parents died in a car accident, Tony inherited Stark Industries and became its CEO, expanding the company's reputation as a leading developer of advanced technology.
